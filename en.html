Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2025

1. Information We Collect

YaMed collects only the minimum data necessary for the app to function, including:

  • Medication data — medicine names, dosages, schedules, and notes that you enter yourself
  • Medication history — records of whether you took your medication on a given day
  • Identity (when signed in) — email address and name from Google Sign-In, used to back up your data to the cloud
  • Personal settings — selected avatar, color theme, and streak badges

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Display your medication list and send timely reminders
  • Calculate your streak to motivate consistent medication adherence
  • Back up and sync your data across devices (signed-in users only)
  • We do not sell, share, or use your data for advertising

3. Data Storage

  • On-device — Medication data and settings are stored locally in AsyncStorage on your device and do not pass through any server
  • Cloud (optional) — If you choose to sign in with Google, your data is encrypted and stored on Firebase (Google Cloud) under Google's security policies

4. Sharing with Third Parties

We do not share your personal data with outside parties, except:

  • Firebase / Google — for Authentication and Cloud Firestore (signed-in users only). See Google's policy at policies.google.com/privacy
  • Legal obligation — if required by a court order or applicable law

5. Push Notifications

YaMed requests permission to send push notifications to remind you to take your medication on time. You can disable notifications at any time in your device's system settings. Turning off notifications has no effect on your medication data.

6. Health Information

YaMed is not a medical application. The medication data and reminders in this app are intended solely to help you remember to take your medicine — they do not constitute medical advice. Please consult a doctor or pharmacist for accurate medication guidance.

7. Your Rights

  • Delete data — You can delete all medications in the app at any time, or uninstall the app to remove on-device data
  • Delete cloud data — Send a request to manglnwza888@gmail.com to have your Firebase data deleted
  • Export data — Contact us if you would like a copy of your data

8. Security

We use Firebase Security Rules to ensure each user can only access their own data. All data transmitted between the app and Firebase is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

9. Children's Privacy

YaMed is not designed for children under the age of 13. If we discover that data has been collected from a child without parental consent, we will delete it immediately.

Data Retention Data stored on the device is retained until the user deletes it within the app or uninstalls the app. Data stored on Firebase is retained while the account remains active. When a user requests account or data deletion, we will delete the associated data within 30 days, unless retention is required by law, for security, or for fraud prevention.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make significant changes to this Privacy Policy, we will notify users through the App Store / Play Store update notes or by updating the date at the top of this page.
